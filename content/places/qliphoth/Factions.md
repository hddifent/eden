> A faction is a group of people dedicated to a cause. There are three main factions in Aletav, The Guild of Aldebrand, Prime Centauri, and The Delvers.

# The Guild of Aldebrand

The main regulators around Aletav and the exploration of Qliphoth. Adventurers must sign up to this guild in order to start exploring the dungeon.

## Departments

The guild consists four departments, **The Sephiroth**, **Search and Expedition Associates (S.E.A.)**, **B.C. Scientia**, and **The Roadweavers**.

### The Sephiroth

> _Archival and Education for the Betterment of Societies_

The Sephiroth is an academia under Aldebrand’s supervision. Its main goal is to index and archive everything about Qliphoth. They are separated into six specialized fields, each lead by an Overseer. Together, these Overseers form a council which governs the workings of their department. Most archives and records are publicly available in the Record Hall.

- **Kether** — _Biological Studies_

	> Documents all biological species found in Qliphoth. They usually study plant-like species themselves while creature-like species are sent to B.C. Scientia for further researches before returning the results for documentation.

- **Yesod** — _Sentient Arcana_

	> Documents all sentient beings that do not have biological properties. These beings have other arcane magic that brings them to life.

- **Binah** — _Ecological and Structural Analysis_

	> Studies the properties of each layer of Qliphoth. Their records include information about dungeon structures and environmental data, along with changes over the years. They also monitor the dungeon for any abnormalities, but nothing has come up so far.

- **Tiphareth** — _Mineralogy and Materials_

	> Indexes every ores and minerals found in Qliphoth, including their site and all of their variants. These samples are then sent to B.C. Scientia for further researches before returning the results for documentation.

- **Netzach** — _Historical Records_

	> Documents and archives every discoveries and expeditions records in Qliphoth over the years.

- **Malkuth** — _Administrative Records_

	> Handles personnel files, accountings, incidents, request logs, etc. If the archival request is not in any other team’s scope, **Malkuth** team will take that job.


### Search and Expedition Associates

Search and Expedition Associates, or S.E.A. in short, is a group of adventurers, archeologists, and expeditors dedicated to map out the entirety of Qliphoth in order to understand its architectures and make future descends easier. The department is split into Search Teams and Expedition Teams. It is lead by a single person called the Trailblazer.

- **Search Teams** explorations are carried out frequently, mostly to complete Qliphoth related requests that remained on the request board for more than a week. They are only permitted to explore the first two layers of Qliphoth.
- **Expedition Teams**, on the other hand, only do occasional deep exploration of Qliphoth to map out the floors. After each exploration, they send a report to the **Netzach** team.

Recently, a new team is formed in cooperation with The Sephiroth to figure out a way to break the barrier on the lowest charted layer. They consider this an urgent matter as it directly blocks the department’s progress.

### B.C. Scientia

> _Pro bono communi, scientia._

B.C. Scientia is a department founded by a group of graduates and hardcore nerds. They are tasked with all researches related to biological masses from the **Kether** team and minerals from the **Tiphareth** team. Additionally, they are actively theorizing about how Qliphoth came to be. Teams are formed based on ongoing projects. Some researches are out of interests while some can be commissioned. Almost every report is available in the Record Hall.

Some notable projects that B.C. Scientia has involvements in includes:

- _Study on Qliphoth Creatures’ Biological Tissue — (Concluded, commissioned by **Kether** team)_
	
	> One of the first projects since the founding of the guild. The research concluded that the creatures found in Qliphoth has similar biological structures to that of the living. However, the cells cannot regenerate. They also don’t require energy to sustain themselves.
	
- _Psychologies of “The Lost” — (Abandoned, internal)_
	
	> After the discovery of new intellectual species inside Qliphoth, a team is formed to study their behaviors. However, after multiple incidents of researchers’ psychological changes, the project was quietly shut down. The report was never publicized.
	
- _Arcane Properties of Cavus — (Concluded, commissioned by **Tiphareth** team)_
	
	> Cavus in one of the most common mineral found in Qliphoth, usually fragile, inert, and has little to no value. However, after a major incident on the 4th floor of Qliphoth involving extractors from Prime Centauri, a research was commissioned. The extractors had found samples of Cavus infused with necrotic magic. After months of research, it is concluded that Cavus can be infused with arcane magic if stimulated, which make them unstable. However, researchers are unable to recreate necrotic-infused Cavus in labs. Further internal researches are currently ongoing.
	
- _Material and Elemental Imbuement — (Ongoing, internal)_
	
	> Following _Arcane Properties of Cavus_, a team is formed to figure out a way to extract arcane magic from infused Cavus without exploding the mineral. They also request material samples from **Tiphareth** team to study their arcane properties.
	
- _Qliphoth Theory on Living Structures — (Ongoing, internal)_
	
	> A research started by a group of conspiracy theorist in the department. They suspect that Qliphoth is able to adapt itself slowly, but their only sources are minor inaccuracies in S.E.A. charted maps between decades. They are dismissed and shunned among other B.C. Scientia researchers.
	
- _Adaptive Molecular Reaction — (Abandoned, commissioned by **Kether** team)_
	
	> Many adventurers had reported a number of creatures inside Qliphoth that can change their body structures. After getting samples from the dungeon, they are immediately sent to B.C. Scientia for researches. However, they could not recreate the phenomenon in lab environments. Those creatures are also never found again, thus the project was shelved indefinitely.
	

### The Roadweavers

The Roadweavers is formed by a group of local and travelling merchants representatives. They mostly operate on their own with some oversight and regulations from the guild. They run the Weavers’ Agora where locals can buy and sell things or directly trade with travelling merchants. Their office is located within the market, where merchants relax, chat, and negotiates. The merchants have their own trading group, each having their specialties; general goods, processed materials, equipment, magic items, and mercenaries.

- General goods are mostly imported from outside Aletav since agricultures around the area are not possible.
- Processed minerals and materials are the main export and income of the city. However, regular metals such as iron, steel, and bronze are imported in small quantities.
- Equipment such as armors and weapons are general, mass-produced stuffs you would find in the capitals. They are sold at slight markups and mostly targeted towards new and aspiring adventurers.
- Magic items are available only on certain time period. They only trade in electrums and nobody knows why. Items sold by these merchants have no specific catalogs; what they have is what they sell. You can also trade by exchanging magic items.
- Mercenaries have to be hired ahead of time, usually at least one week. Their fees are based on their skill level, ranging from complete newbies to veterans on top of their game.

## General Duties

The general duties of the guild includes but not limited to:

- **Town Keeping** — Manages the town’s request board. These requests don’t have to be done by adventurers; everyone can take the requests and complete it for rewards, usually gold pieces.
- **Town Guardians** — Manages a group of trained guard ands soldiers around the town. They also have dedicated units inside Qliphoth to help with early-floor mineral extractions.
- **Permit Issuing** — The guild issues exploration and extractions permits for business inside Qliphoth.
	- Exploration permits have four levels of permissions: Restricted, Shallow, Deep, and Uncharted.
		- Restricted Exploration permits are issued to scholars and the general public. — It grants free access to the first floor of Qliphoth and guided access down to the 3rd floor.
		- Shallow Exploration permits are issued to graded adventurers, members of S.E.A., and the **Binah** team of The Sephiroth. — It grants access to the first two layers of Qliphoth.
		- Deep Exploration permits are issued to Quartz or above graded adventurers and teams lead by senior expeditor of S.E.A. — It grants access to all known layers of Qliphoth.
		- Uncharted Exploration permits are issued on special occasions to expedition teams assigned by the Trailblazer of S.E.A. — It grants access to layers below charted territories of Qliphoth.
	- Extraction permits only allow early-floor mineral extractions.
	- These permits have an expiration date.
- **Adventurer Grading** — Ranking adventurers’ capabilities through trials and challenges.
	- There are six ranks each adventurers can achieve: Talc, Calcite, Apatite, Quartz, Topaz, and Diamond.

# Prime Centauri

A group of outsiders that sees the potential investments that can be made from “everything” found inside Qliphoth. Together, they form an extraction operation force called Prime Centauri, funded and owned privately. They operate separately from The Guild of Aldebrand, technically not above them. They sure act like the one in charge, though. With the power of capitalism and massive wealth on their side, they’re unstoppable.

## Divisions

The operation is ruled by The **Aurum** Committees, a board of influential executives who rarely sets foot in Aletav themselves. Some employees don’t even know their identities. Beneath them are the four core divisions: **Natrium**, **Kalium**, **Argentum**, and **Hydragyrum**.

- The **Natrium** division handles the labor works; mining, extracting, refining, and logistics falls into their work scope. The lowest of the bunch in **Aurum**’s eyes, being seen as replaceable and a necessary sacrifice for a “greater good”.
- The **Kalium** division is Prime Centauri’s internal research team. They mainly focus on mining and extraction technologies. There also exists some side projects on Qliphoth exploration, aiming to understand and harness the source of all minerals. Lately though, a new secret project on Cavus was created that some members of the division don’t even know about. Information are highly confidential.
- The **Argentum** division is there to upkeep public relations with the people of Aletav and occasionally negotiate with the guild. Although, both they and the guild know that this is merely for show. They are easily the most dramatic bunch. When there’s a problem they could throw someone else under the bus, their scapegoat’s reputation never recovers again.
- The **Hydragyrum** division is the brain of the operation full of cut-throat strategists who are not afraid to get their hands dirty. The division is created with the only aim to maximize profitability. They handle all acquisition jobs, legal issues, and occasional “liquidation” jobs. Nobody really trust them, not even the other divisions. Their meetings are the one you don’t want to be a part of the guestlist.

## Important Events

### Necro-infused Cavus Explosion

The incident happened while members of the **Natrium** division are carrying an extraction task on the 4th floor of Qliphoth. A massive explosion erupts from one of the caverns, killing 3 members and leaving 10 injured, all of which are reported dead a week after.

It was discovered that the explosion contains high presence of arcane energy. After an internal investigation along with the **Tiphareth** team from The Sephiroth, they found pieces of Cavus infused with necrotic magic. It turns out the mineral is found deep inside the walls of Qliphoth which the **Natrium** team dug into without the guild’s permissions. However, they denied any wrongdoings, citing loopholes in the extraction permit.

In the end, to avoid public scrutiny, a mutual agreement was made between the two factions. Prime Centauri cancelled their extraction plans on the 4th floor, sealed off the cavern, and paid “hush money” to the, at the time, struggling guild. The Guild of Aldebrand added a new group of guards to station inside Qliphoth and rewrote their permit clauses. The story is quickly suppressed and buried by **Argentum** division.

### Raid on Swarm #4761

The raid is a coordinated attack believed to have been organized The Delvers. Although, there are no direct proof or concrete evidence. The logistic drones have been shot down by an unknown source, hitting most of the cargos. The lost values are estimated to be about 100,000 gold pieces. The crash site shows no wreckages nor anything deemed salvageable.

The story was covered as drone malfunction, and their reputation took a huge blow in the public perception. The investigation is still ongoing quietly and secretly by **Hydragyrum** division.

# The Delvers

A group of people with one shared dedicated cause, to get to the bottom of Qliphoth. They are ruled by a sole leader, all gathered in a secluded area in the darkest corner of Aletav. They see Aldebrand as ineffective group of old minds, stuck in age old traditions and bureaucracies. Yet, they are so pretentious, thinking that the way they rule the town are for the best to everyone. As for Prime Centauri, they deemed them to be untrustworthy, filled with people with no interests but themselves. To The Delvers, those are the forces that are holding back the truth, whether intentionally or not.

Some members have lost their family, their livelihood, their heart and blood, all to Qliphoth. They all have their individual reasons; revenge, redemption, forgiveness, tranquility, _finality_. They all seek answers for different questions, but all of them means going down into the dungeon’s core. The guild views them as a threat to public safety due to their countless reckless actions. As a result, bounties were put on all of them, painting them as the outlaws.

The group now lives in secrecy, away from all people, in their small hideout. The only ways for them to survive under these circumstances are to scavenge everything by any means necessary and trade with their connections.
