---
title: Prime Centauri
---
A group of outsiders that sees the potential investments that can be made from "everything" found inside [[qliphoth|Qliphoth]]. Together, they form an extraction operation force called Prime Centauri, funded and owned privately. They operate separately from [[aldebrand|The Guild of Aldebrand]], technically not above them. They sure act like the one in charge, though. With the power of capitalism and massive wealth on their side, they’re unstoppable.

## Divisions

The operation is ruled by The **Aurum** Committees, a board of influential executives who rarely sets foot in [[aletav|Aletav]] themselves. Some employees don’t even know their identities. Beneath them are the four core divisions: **Natrium**, **Kalium**, **Argentum**, and **Hydragyrum**.

- The **Natrium** division handles the labor works; mining, extracting, refining, and logistics falls into their work scope. The lowest of the bunch in **Aurum**’s eyes, being seen as replaceable and a necessary sacrifice for a "greater good".
- The **Kalium** division is Prime Centauri’s internal research team. They mainly focus on mining and extraction technologies. There also exists some side projects on Qliphoth exploration, aiming to understand and harness the source of all minerals. Lately though, a new secret project on Cavus was created that some members of the division don’t even know about. Information are highly confidential.
- The **Argentum** division is there to upkeep public relations with the people of Aletav and occasionally negotiate with the guild. Although, both they and the guild know that this is merely for show. They are easily the most dramatic bunch. When there’s a problem they could throw someone else under the bus, their scapegoat’s reputation never recovers again.
- The **Hydragyrum** division is the brain of the operation full of cut-throat strategists who are not afraid to get their hands dirty. The division is created with the only aim to maximize profitability. They handle all acquisition jobs, legal issues, and occasional "liquidation" jobs. Nobody really trust them, not even the other divisions. Their meetings are the one you don’t want to be a part of the guestlist.

## Important Events

### Necro-infused Cavus Explosion

The incident happened while members of the **Natrium** division are carrying an extraction task on the 4th floor of Qliphoth. A massive explosion erupts from one of the caverns, killing 3 members and leaving 10 injured, all of which are reported dead a week after.

It was discovered that the explosion contains high presence of arcane energy. After an internal investigation along with the **Tiphareth** team from The Sephiroth, they found pieces of Cavus infused with necrotic magic. It turns out the mineral is found deep inside the walls of Qliphoth which the **Natrium** team dug into without the guild’s permissions. However, they denied any wrongdoings, citing loopholes in the extraction permit.

In the end, to avoid public scrutiny, a mutual agreement was made between the two factions. Prime Centauri cancelled their extraction plans on the 4th floor, sealed off the cavern, and paid "hush money" to the, at the time, struggling guild. The Guild of Aldebrand added a new group of guards to station inside Qliphoth and rewrote their permit clauses. The story is quickly suppressed and buried by **Argentum** division.

### Raid on Swarm #4761

The raid is a coordinated attack believed to have been organized [[the-delvers|The Delvers]]. Although, there are no direct proof or concrete evidence. The logistic drones have been shot down by an unknown source, hitting most of the cargos. The lost values are estimated to be about 100,000 gold pieces. The crash site shows no wreckages nor anything deemed salvageable.

The story was covered as drone malfunction, and their reputation took a huge blow in the public perception. The investigation is still ongoing quietly and secretly by **Hydragyrum** division.
