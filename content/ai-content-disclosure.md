title: AI Content Disclosure
---
The creator and contributors of Eden ("the Team", "we", "our", or "us") hereby pledge to disclose all usages of generative AI, no matter how small it is.

## Our AI Usage Guidelines

We hereby declare our AI usage guidelines:

- We shall **never** use generative AI to generate contents such as full writings, images, audios, and other contents that are deemed creative material. Such usages are violations of others' copyrights as the training data are sourced unethically without permissions.
- We **may** use generative AI to restructure sentences and check for grammatical errors.
- We **may** use generative AI as part of general researching, of which they must be manually cited and verify later to ensure correctness.
- We will **always** disclose the usage of any AI-generated contents that appear in the site, no matter the amount contributed.

Any violations or failure to comply with the guidelines will bar the violator from ever contributing to this site again. Any contents that fail to comply with the guidelines will be removed from the site.
