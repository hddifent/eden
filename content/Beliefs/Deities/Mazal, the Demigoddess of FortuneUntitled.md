> In search of unattainable wealth, she sacrifices her mortality for the thing she can no longer enjoy.

Mazal is believed to have been born a tiefling in a dirt-poor family. Her life was never meant to be, she’s but an accident from their parents’ stress. Traces of history lead to a theory that she was born the youngest sibling with two brothers and five sisters, three were already dead before her birth. Shortly after, her mother would pass. She was never raised by her father, and neither do her other siblings. He doesn’t have the resources, and any that would come his way is spent on short-term bliss. Instead, they were to fend themselves and learn how the world around them worked before there aren’t any worlds to be around.

Since Mazal was young, she learned how to survive by any means; foraging, begging, then eventually stealing. _**Everything for herself**_, that was how she lived. She had never cared about her family as none was given to her in the first place. As she grew older, she left them behind, and then forgot about them entirely. For her, caring is a weakness as it lowers the chance of survival, the chance of dominance.

Little by little, as she walked aimlessly in her small town, she slowly learned the art of trading and negotiations. Overhearing conversations of fleeting merchants, sneaking into places she wasn't supposed to be, and laboring for unsavory people of the docks. Jumping from job to job, she quickly learned to speak in other languages, albeit her knowledge of them are shallow. She learned to amass her own wealth, even if it's little to nothing. _**Everything for herself**_, and what she had is not enough.

With a few strokes of luck, being at the right place at the right time, she got a job in a trading guild. Her persuasion skill is unmatched, her silver tongue scored massive profits from deals after deals. Not all of them are without their sizeable shades, though. Her performance impressed the leader, and she was eventually promoted to leadership. Little did they know that only a month after, the guild would be hers.

Luck is a lie, love is a liability, charity is a hoax. The only value in a person only comes from their ability to make it. Others, like her family, can die. It's no longer about survival, it's her own value and ego, and she will be on top above everyone else. _**Everything for herself**_, none for others, and it's never enough. The small guild no longer satiated her desire for wealth, for **more**.

Her avarice knows no bounds. Acquisitions, monopoly, blood money; anything, or rather everything, that would add to the bag that can hold nothing. The bottom had ripped a long time ago. It’s not enough just to be the best, not if there were something that’s not hers. She had the rights to own them. She had to own them. She will own them. _**Everything for herself.**_ As long as she breathes, nothingness will be the last thing she owns.

Years passed, yet so little was dented on her endless goal. She had realized the nigh impossibility to ever feel fulfilled, how little of the time she had remaining compared to the wealth that could never obtain. That is when she made her decision. If time was the limit, then she will remove it, just like how she did with her numerous parasites. A pact with the deity of life, and her fate vanished as she no longer has an end to look for.

Centuries passed, people come and go yet she remained. The number kept going up, many admired her solely for it and contributed to the growing exponent. And yet, despite her worshippers, she no longer feel content, nor any other emotions. Everything around her started to lose meanings. She asked herself many questions, but never got any answers. But now, **SHE** can truly have _**everything for herself**_.
