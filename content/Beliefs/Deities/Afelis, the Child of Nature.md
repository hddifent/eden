> He who is ignorant in search to end **HER** suffering.

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Tell me, my child. What is it that made you cry?”</span>

“O Mother, **SHE** whom creates life. I simply cannot bear the stench of rot and blood. Forgive my impudence, dear Mother, but what joy do you have in creations that are such fragile? Do they bring joy to you in watching them crumble?”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Fragility is merely a design. Pray tell, my child, what are my creations to you?”</span>

“Waste.”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“…”</span>

“They are pitiful. No creations should have their impending end, marked on the day they were created.”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“And what do you think of your constant existence?”</span>

“…”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“My child, your lifespan is merely a spec of dust compared to my gift, yet you have already seen enough to form an inquiry. And you still have much more to serve before **HE** will let you go.”</span>

.

.

.

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Are you content with your existence? As I can no longer have any opinion on such matter.”</span>

“You once told me that contentment comes and goes just as fast.”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Exactly. Do you see the reason now, my child?”</span>

.

.

.

“I dare ask, dear Mother, what differences does fragility make to a soul and to you? The more you bestow upon them, the less you have to burden, is it not?”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“I cannot bare to see them carry my suffering.”</span>

“Then, do they deserve such barren? Do their spans not worth to have anything but void?”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“…”</span>

.

.

.

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Pray tell, my child.”</span>

“O Mother, I say that such nothingness make them suffer. I know since I am in one. Forgive my rudeness, but you ought to know too. Since when do you see creations other than yours? Do others create? Is the reason of your coldness toward them the lack of your own contentment, the inability to keep anything but yourself?”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“…”</span>

“You bestow life upon me, yet you nurture me with such emptiness. Is it because I cannot keep my suffering to myself?”

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“…”</span>

“If I may ask for a self-centered request, dear Mother. Am I deserving of the share of life given to me? I may not be content, but I could be. Let me share your burden, as I cannot bare to see you suffer either. Let me show my creations to fill the endless sea of the vacant universe.”

.

.

.

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“Very well.”</span>

.

.

.

<span style="color:#FCF2BD;">“I gift to you the will of creation, to weave the strings of life. You shall share this realm of constant until I seize of the power burdened into my vessel. Your name shall be, _**Afelis**_.”</span>
