# Mazal, the Demigoddess of Fortune

> In search of unattainable wealth, she sacrifices her mortality for the thing she can no longer enjoy.

Mazal is believed to have been born a tiefling in a dirt-poor family. Her life was never meant to be, she’s but an accident from their parents’ stress. Traces of history lead to a theory that she was born the youngest sibling with two brothers and five sisters, three were already dead before her birth. Shortly after, her mother would pass. She was never raised by her father, and neither do her other siblings. He doesn’t have the resources, and any that would come his way is spent on short-term bliss. Instead, they are to fend themselves and learn how the world around them work before there aren’t any worlds to be around.

Since Mazal was young, she learned how to survive by any means; foraging, begging, then eventually stealing. _**Everything for herself**_, that was how she lived. She had never cared about her family as none was given to her in the first place. As she grows older, she left them behind, and then forgets about them entirely. For her, caring is a weakness as it lowers the chance of survival, the chance of dominance.

Little by little, as she walked aimlessly in her small town, she slowly learned the art of trading and negotiations. Overhearing conversations of fleeting merchants, sneaking into places she wasn't supposed to be, and laboring for unsavory people of the docks. Jumping from job to job, she quickly learned to speak in other languages, albeit her knowledge of them are shallow. She learned to amass her own wealth, even if it's little to nothing. _**Everything for herself**_, and what she currently have is not enough.

With a few stroke of luck, being at the right place at the right time, she got a job in a trading guild. Her persuasion skill is unmatched, her silver tongue scored massive profits from deals after deals. Not all of them are without blood, though. Her performance impressed the leader, and she was eventually promoted to leadership. Little did they know that only a month after, the guild would be hers.
