## What is Eden?

Eden is a homebrew [Dungeons and Dragons (D&D)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en?msockid=2dada1f0e48261631f73b78ce52b6081) setting, created by yours truly.

## Why is Eden?

The idea started brewing as soon as I read the official D&D settings. My actual thoughts are "Ain't no way I'ma read all that and understand.", so I decided to create one myself. Worldbuilding couldn't be *that* hard, right?

*Oh, you naïve bastard...*

Originally created as a *simple location name* to fit a single point of interest, the world quickly expands as I realized "Holy sh\*t, the amount of worldbuilding I have to do. F\*ck, f\*ck, f\*ck."

Does it need to be this big for an amateur campaign of D&D that is never going to last more than 3 sessions? Yes.

Does it feel good? F\*ck, yes.
