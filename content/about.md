## What is Eden?

Eden is a homebrew [Dungeons and Dragons (D&D)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en?msockid=2dada1f0e48261631f73b78ce52b6081) setting, created by yours truly. This pastime project kickstarted in August 2025 but really gained momentum in January 2026. *(We'll see how long this lasts...)*

## Why is Eden?

The idea started brewing as soon as I read the official D&D settings. My actual thoughts were *"Ain't no way I'ma read all that and understand."*, so I decided to create one myself. Worldbuilding couldn't be *that* hard, right?

*Oh, you naïve bastard...*

Originally created as a *simple location name* to fit a single point of interest, the world quickly expands as I realized *"Holy sh\*t, the amount of worldbuilding I have to do. F\*ck, f\*ck, f\*ck."*

Does it need to be this big for an amateur campaign of D&D that is never going to last more than 3 sessions? Yes.

Does it feel good? **F\*ck, yes**.

## How is Eden?

Eden is slowly being made as I think of more stuffs. There are three [[poi/|regions]] planned in mind, [[poi/qliphoth/|one]] is currently being developed for a campaign. I do hope to have them finish before I die.

I do not consider myself to be a good writer. This place is more like a training ground for me, or perhaps an experiment lab to try something new. If the writings are inconsistent, it's probably because of that. ~~(Or maybe I just suck, I dunno.)~~
